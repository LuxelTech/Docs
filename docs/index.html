01 Drone photogrammetry & mapping

Every surface, measured.

Automated drone flights capture hundreds of overlapping, geotagged images of a building and its site. Photogrammetry turns them into accurate, measurable 3D data, without scaffolding, lifts or anyone on the roof.

  • 3D models Textured meshes and point clouds of the whole structure.
  • Orthomosaics Map-accurate aerial images of roofs and sites.
  • Measurements Roof areas, heights, volumes and distances.
  • Documentation Facade and roof condition, construction progress and closeout.

02 Thermography

Heat tells the story.

Calibrated infrared cameras, handheld and drone-mounted, map the surface temperatures of roofs and facades. Problems the eye can't see show up as clear thermal patterns, long before they turn into repairs.

  • Roofs Wet insulation under flat roofs, found before leaks spread.
  • Envelope Missing insulation, thermal bridges and air leakage.
  • Electrical Overheating breakers, connections and equipment.
  • Reports Radiometric images with temperatures, locations and findings.

04 How it works

From flight plan to report.

Every survey follows the same four steps, so you know what happens on site and what you receive at the end.

  1. STEP 01

    Plan the flight

    We map the site and its surroundings, then plan an automated flight path, altitude and camera settings for the building.

  2. STEP 02

    Fly and capture

    The drone flies the planned route, capturing overlapping photos and calibrated thermal images. No scaffolding, no lifts, no one on the roof.

  3. STEP 03

    Process

    The images become point clouds, 3D models and orthomosaics; the thermal images are checked and analysed.

  4. STEP 04

    Deliver the report

    You receive the models, maps and a clear report: each finding with its location, thermal image, temperature and what it means.
